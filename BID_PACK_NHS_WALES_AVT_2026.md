## 1. Executive bid position

### Recommendation: CONDITIONAL GO / immediate eligibility gate

The opportunity is strategically attractive because it is an open framework for AVT and explicitly describes capabilities including ambient capture, speech-to-text, generative-AI summarisation, structured coding, template population, review/approval workflows, EMIS Web integration, and reporting.

However, the current company evidence available to this build shows a digital-product/automation proposition rather than a verified clinical AVT product. The public company site currently presents conversion, lead capture, AI automation and operational systems, with products at £29, £49 and £199. It does **not** establish an AVT clinical product, NHS deployment history, clinical safety case, DTAC compliance, cyber assurance, EMIS integration, or NHS AVT registry status. These must not be invented.

**Decision rule:** proceed to full tender response only if the supplier can evidence the mandatory eligibility/technical requirements in the tender pack or can lawfully bid with an eligible AVT product/partner arrangement. Otherwise, the correct commercial move is to pursue an AVT partnership/subcontractor route and/or the NHS England AVT supplier registry while using this framework as the target account.

## 4. Win themes

### WT1 — Safe clinical augmentation, not autonomous clinical decision-making
Position AVT as a clinician-support capability. Outputs are drafts for review and approval, with clear accountability and auditability.

### WT2 — Measurable administrative time reduction
Propose measurement around documentation time, turnaround, completeness, clinician acceptance/rework and user adoption. Do not state savings percentages without evidence.

### WT3 — Interoperability-first delivery
Make EMIS Web integration and standards-based interfaces a first-class workstream. Do not promise an integration until technically verified.

### WT4 — Governance designed into the product
Build privacy, consent, security, clinical safety, model-change control, audit logging and human review into the service rather than treating them as post-launch paperwork.

### WT5 — Scalable national framework delivery
Offer a repeatable onboarding model: assurance → configuration → pilot → measured rollout → support → continuous monitoring.

## 6. Technical response architecture

### 6.1 Reference architecture

1. **Capture layer** — approved clinical device/browser/mobile capture as permitted by the tender.
2. **Secure transport** — encrypted transmission with authenticated access.
3. **Speech processing** — transcription with documented quality measurement.
4. **AI transformation** — summarisation/structuring against approved templates and use cases.
5. **Safety controls** — confidence/exception handling, prohibited-content controls where applicable, human review and approval.
6. **Clinical-system integration** — EMIS Web/integration layer subject to buyer-approved interface arrangements.
7. **Audit layer** — access, processing, changes and approvals logged appropriately.
8. **Reporting** — adoption, quality, service and outcome metrics.

## 8. Pricing model

**Do not insert invented prices into the tender.** Use the buyer's official pricing schedule and align all prices to its units, assumptions and term.

Recommended commercial structure for internal modelling:

| Cost component | Pricing basis | Internal control |
|---|---|---|
| Platform licence | Per clinician / user / site / agreed unit | Calculate fully loaded gross margin |
| Implementation | One-off mobilisation/integration | Include technical + clinical + IG effort |
| Training | Per cohort/site or included | State assumptions |
| Support | Included or tiered service | Define SLA cost |
| Integration | Fixed/variable | Price only after interface scope confirmed |
| Reporting/analytics | Included or tiered | Avoid double charging for core assurance |
| Optional services | Clearly separated | Buyer approval required |

### Pricing guardrails

- Never price below the verified cost-to-serve.
- Separate one-off implementation from recurring licence revenue.
- Include cloud/model/API usage costs.
- Include support and clinical/technical assurance overhead.
- Model volume tiers for national scaling.
- Ensure every assumption is visible in the tender schedule.
