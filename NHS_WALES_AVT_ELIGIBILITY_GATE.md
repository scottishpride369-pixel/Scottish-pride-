## Decision
**Current recommendation: PARTNER BID / HOLD DIRECT BID.**

Reason: the public notice confirms the framework scope includes AVT, generative AI summarisation, clinical coding, workflows and EMIS Web integration, but the connected company evidence does not establish those capabilities or NHS clinical evidence. The public notice also states the full bidding and technical documents are in eTenderWales/Jaggaer. We must not invent missing evidence.

A direct bid becomes viable only after the full ITT is retrieved and every mandatory condition is evidenced. A partner bid is the preferred route if an established AVT/clinical supplier can provide the product, NHS references, EMIS integration and clinical/assurance evidence while we contribute AI/automation, implementation, workflow, analytics or delivery capability.

## Critical blocker sequence
1. Human owner retrieves ITT 126244 from eTenderWales/Jaggaer.
2. Procurement lead extracts every mandatory pass/fail condition and scoring question.
3. Technical lead maps product capability against each condition.
4. Clinical/IG/security lead supplies required assurance evidence.
5. Partnerships lead identifies an eligible AVT supplier with NHS references and EMIS capability.
6. Commercial lead completes the actual tender pricing schedule; no invented prices.
7. Legal/procurement owner approves submission terms and declarations.
8. Final bid/no-bid gate before portal submission.
