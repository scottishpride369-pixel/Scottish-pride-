# NHS Wales Ambient Voice Technology — Bid Master Pack

**Buyer:** Digital Health & Care Wales (DHCW)
**Opportunity:** Ambient Voice Technology (AVT) Solutions
**Reference:** PRJ-1331 / ITT 126244
**Framework value:** £10,000,000 estimated, excluding VAT
**Current deadline:** 17 September 2026, 12:00 (amended notice)
**Enquiry deadline:** 4 September 2026, 23:59

## 1. Bid position

Status: **BID PREPARATION — NOT SUBMITTED**

The public notice confirms an Open Framework Agreement for AVT solutions supporting NHS Wales organisations and GMS providers. The scope includes ambient voice capture, real-time speech-to-text, generative-AI summarisation, suggested structured clinical coding, intelligent summarisation, automated template population, review/approval workflows, EMIS Web integration, insights and reporting.

## 2. Critical caveat

The full ITT documents and scoring methodology are held in the eTenderWales/Jaggaer portal. This pack must not be treated as a completed compliant tender response until the portal documents are obtained and reviewed.

Do not claim healthcare deployments, certifications, integrations, security accreditations, clinical outcomes, personnel qualifications, references, insurance levels or compliance status unless documentary evidence exists.

## 3. Win strategy

Lead with:
- measurable reduction in clinical documentation burden;
- human review and approval before clinical record use;
- privacy/security-by-design;
- auditable AI outputs and change history;
- interoperability and practical workflow integration;
- rapid, controlled implementation and training;
- transparent performance measurement.

## 4. Proposed technical response structure

### A. Executive response
Position the supplier as an implementation-focused technology partner capable of delivering a controlled AVT workflow from capture through transcription, summarisation, structured output, human review and approved record insertion.

### B. Clinical workflow
1. Consent and session initiation.
2. Secure audio capture.
3. Speech-to-text processing.
4. AI summarisation/structured extraction.
5. Clinician review and correction.
6. Approved output transferred to the relevant workflow/system.
7. Audit and reporting.

### C. Safety controls
- Human-in-the-loop approval.
- No autonomous clinical decision-making.
- Clear AI-generated provenance.
- Configurable retention/deletion.
- Access controls and audit logging.
- Incident and escalation process.
- Accuracy monitoring and quality assurance.

### D. Integration
The public notice identifies EMIS Web as the current GP-system integration requirement. The final response must provide verified technical evidence for the actual integration method proposed.

### E. Security and data protection
Prepare an evidence pack covering:
- UK GDPR/data-protection roles;
- DPIA approach;
- data residency;
- encryption in transit/at rest;
- identity/access management;
- audit logging;
- vulnerability management;
- incident response;
- supplier/subprocessor controls;
- retention/deletion;
- business continuity and disaster recovery.

Insert only verified certifications and policies.
