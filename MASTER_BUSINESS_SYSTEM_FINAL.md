## 8. Autonomous operating model
Agents/workflows may operate continuously where technically and legally permitted.

### Monitor
Traffic, leads, checkout, orders, inventory, site uptime, errors, analytics, automation failures, domain enquiries and asset status.

### Act
Route leads, update CRM/project records, send permitted notifications, generate routine reports, prepare content, flag issues, update dashboards and create follow-up tasks.

### Human approval gates
Required for:
- contracts and legal commitments
- pricing changes outside approved rules
- spending/ad budget changes
- customer refunds outside policy
- partner commitments
- publication of sensitive claims
- payment/account security changes
- irreversible infrastructure or domain actions
